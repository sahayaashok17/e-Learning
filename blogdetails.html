Why Swift UI Should Be on the Radar of Every Mobile Developer

TOTC is a platform that allows educators to create online classes where they can store course materials, manage assignments, quizzes and exams, monitor due dates, grade results, and provide students with feedback — all in one place.

Digital learning platforms are changing the way educators and students connect. By bringing lessons, learning resources, assignments, and communication tools together, an online classroom can make learning easier to organize and access.

TOTC is designed to support this experience. Educators can organize course materials, keep track of important dates, review student work, and share feedback from one convenient learning environment. Students can use the platform to follow their courses and stay informed about their progress.

A well-designed learning experience should be simple to navigate, accessible across devices, and focused on helping learners spend more time learning. Clear course information and organized resources help make that experience more productive for everyone.
